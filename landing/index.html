What it does

Built for Cypriot Greek
from first principles.

Stuff a standard Greek keyboard leaves out, and the daily annoyances it doesn't.

✦

Greeklish autocorrect

Type "kalimera" — get "καλημέρα". Type "Lefkosia" — get "Λευκωσία". Built on a real Cypriot dictionary, not a transliteration table.

σ̆

Cypriot characters

σ̆ ζ̆ ξ̆ ψ̆ for the sounds standard Greek doesn't have. A dedicated breve key, in the right place. Final-σ flips to ς automatically.

´

Tonos done right

The keyboard places the tonos for multi-syllable words while you type. No more accent menus, no more long-press combos.

Setup

30 seconds.

Three taps in Settings, and the keyboard is yours wherever iOS lets you type — Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, Notes, Twitter, Slack.

  1. 1

    Open the app

    Tap "Install in 3 taps" — it'll deep-link straight to the Keyboards screen in iOS Settings.

  2. 2

    Add the keyboard

    Tap Add New Keyboard → Κυπριακά.

  3. 3

    Tap 🌐 anywhere

    In any app, tap the globe icon on your keyboard to switch to Cypriot. That's it.

Made in Cyprus

A keyboard, by a Cypriot, for the language we actually speak.

Standard Greek keyboards don't have the characters Cypriot Greek uses every day. They don't know that "Lefkosia" should auto-correct to "Λευκωσία", or that "je" is "τζαι". This one does.

Built on top of a Cypriot Greek dictionary curated by Dr Spyros Armostis (University of Cyprus), with phonetic refinements from Aceras Anthropophorum, on the KeyboardKit framework by Daniel Saidi.

Από την Κύπρο

Ένα πληκτρολόγιο, από Κύπριο, για τη γλώσσα που πραγματικά μιλάμε.

Τα κοινά Ελληνικά πληκτρολόγια δεν έχουν τους χαρακτήρες που χρησιμοποιούμε στα Κυπριακά. Δεν ξέρουν ότι το "Lefkosia" είναι "Λευκωσία", ή ότι το "je" είναι "τζαι". Αυτό ξέρει.

Βασισμένο στο Κυπριακό λεξικό του Δρ Σπύρου Αρμοστή (Πανεπιστήμιο Κύπρου), με φωνητικές διορθώσεις από τον Aceras Anthropophorum, στο framework KeyboardKit του Daniel Saidi.
