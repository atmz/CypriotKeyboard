## Description (max 4000)

```
Cypriot Keyboard turns "Lefkosia" into "Λευκωσία". Type the way you naturally text — in Greeklish, English-letter shorthand, or full Greek — and let the keyboard do the work.

Built specifically for Cypriot Greek, with the characters and conventions that standard Greek keyboards leave out:

  • Greeklish autocorrect — type "kalimera", get "καλημέρα"; type "Lefkosia", get "Λευκωσία".
  • Cypriot characters — σ̆ ζ̆ ξ̆ ψ̆ for the sounds standard Greek doesn't have, on a dedicated breve key.
  • Smart accents — the keyboard adds the right tonos for you, on multi-syllable words.
  • Final-σ done right — σ in the middle of a word, ς at the end, automatically.
  • Works everywhere iOS keyboards do — Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, Notes, anywhere you can type.

Privacy:
  • No internet access — the keyboard works fully offline.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no telemetry.
  • Open Access is OFF — your keystrokes never leave the device.

Made in Cyprus by Alex Toumazis. Built on the Cypriot Greek dictionary curated by Dr Spyros Armostis (University of Cyprus), on the KeyboardKit framework by Daniel Saidi.

Free, no ads, no in-app purchases. Just a keyboard that gets the language right.
```
