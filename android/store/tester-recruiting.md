## English (WhatsApp / Reddit)

Hey! 🇨🇾 I built the first **Cypriot Greek keyboard** for Android — type
"tziai" and it becomes **τζ̆αι**; it knows ίντα, λαλώ, εν and the rest of
the dialect words no standard Greek keyboard has. Free, open-source, no
ads, no internet permission.

Google Play requires 12 testers before it can go public, and I need your
help — takes 2 minutes:

1. Join the tester group: https://groups.google.com/g/cypriot-keyboard-testers
2. Tap "Become a tester": <OPTIN_LINK>
3. Install it from the Play Store (link shown in step 2)
4. Settings → System → Keyboard → On-screen keyboards → enable
   "Cypriot Keyboard"

⚠️ The catch: keep it installed for 2 weeks (that's how Google counts a
tester) — and actually type with it now and then! Any bugs or weird
suggestions, send them my way 🙏
