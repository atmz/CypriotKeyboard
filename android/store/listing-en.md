## Full description (4000 chars max)
The first keyboard built specifically for **Cypriot Greek** — including the
sounds standard Greek doesn't have. Type the way Cypriots actually speak
and write.

### Two ways to type
- **Greek layout:** standard Cypriot Greek alphabet, with the breve marks
  (σ̆, ζ̆, τσ̆) that distinguish Cypriot phonology from standard Greek.
  Long-press any vowel for accent variants (ά, έ, ή, …); long-press the
  consonants for breve forms.
- **Greeklish layout:** type Latin letters and watch them turn into the
  right Cypriot Greek words. "kalimera" becomes καλημέρα. "tziai" becomes
  τζ̆αι. "shieri" becomes σ̆ιερι. Tap 🔄 to swap between Greek and Latin
  rows whenever you want.

### Autocorrect that knows Cypriot Greek
Backed by a 49 MB dictionary of Cypriot Greek words with their proper
diacritics — including dialect words (ίντα, μιτά, λαλώ, εν) that no
standard Greek dictionary contains. When you tap space, the highlighted
correction is applied. If you really meant what you typed, tap backspace
once and space stays untouched.

### Smart Greek touches
- Final sigma is automatic: type σ at the end of a word, it becomes ς.
  Keep typing and it goes back to σ.
- Tonos accents (΄), dialytika (¨), and the combined tonos-dialytika (΅)
  apply to the previous vowel only when the combination is valid.

### Privacy by design
Nothing leaves your device. The app has no internet permission. No
analytics, no telemetry, no ads. Your keystrokes stay between you and the
text field. Open-source under GPL-3.0.
